FLUFFY EARS CAFE (MILKTEA ORDERING SYSTEM) USER INSTRUCTION MANUAL
1.	Open the application.
2.	On the welcome screen, click the "Click Here to Order" button to start ordering.
3.	Select your preferred order type:
4.	Dine In or Takeout
5.	Choose your desired milk tea flavor from the menu.
6.	Customize your order by selecting:
7.	Size
8.	Sugar Level
9.	Add-ons (if available)
10.	Quantity
11.	Review your selected items and click the Proceed button.
12.	At the checkout page, verify your order details.
13.	Select your preferred payment method.
14.	Confirm your order.
15.	The system will generate and display a receipt containing the order summary and total amount.
16.	Click Return to Start if you wish to place another order.
System Features:
•	Dine In and Takeout Ordering
•	Milk Tea Menu Selection
•	Order Customization
•	Checkout Processing
•	Payment Selection
•	Receipt Generation
